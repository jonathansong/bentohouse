Welcome to Bento House

Bento House is a restaurant where everything revolves around passion for food. We work with fresh products and can therefore adjust the ingredients in the dishes according to your needs. In the restaurant you can enjoy refined Chinese cuisine. Whether it's a romantic dinner for two, a family dinner or a business meeting, you can come to us.

With extensive experience both in the kitchen and in the dining room, we will take care of you down to the smallest details so that you not only leave with a full stomach but also with a happy feeling.
